your attention moves to the future, bracing for loss you expect
your attention moves to the past, dwelling in loss you relive
your attention moves to the present, finding nothing missing

as you leave the present you lose the details of the moment. your memory and imagination lose resolution and become sketches. they're incomplete. you fill them in with stories you tell yourself. 
and you mistake them for photographs.
