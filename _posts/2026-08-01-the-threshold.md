blue bleeds into the sky 
holding onto the threads the world wasn't there to judge 
sharing ideas and secrets and stories that only darkness has room for
room shared with those who wouldn't let the morning slip 
the night is still here, yours 
but now put to rest by birdsong's lullaby 


sunrise splits the night 
a breath. grasping for air before it becomes someone else's 
revealing the trails that the light makes room for 
room shared with those who couldn't let go of the night 
the morning is now here, yours 
and now rallied by dawn's chorus
