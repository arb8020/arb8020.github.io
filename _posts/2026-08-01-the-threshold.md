title: "the threshold"
date: 2026-08-01
slug: the-threshold
category: poem
---

<div class="poem-columns">
<div class="stanza">
blue bleeds into the sky<br>
holding onto the threads the world wasn't there to judge<br>
sharing ideas and secrets and stories that only darkness has room for<br>
room shared with those who wouldn't let the morning slip<br>
the night is still here, yours<br>
but now put to rest by birdsong's lullaby
</div>
<div class="stanza">
sunrise splits the night<br>
a breath. grasping for air before it becomes someone else's<br>
revealing the trails that the light makes room for<br>
room shared with those who couldn't let go of the night<br>
the morning is now here, yours<br>
and now rallied by dawn's chorus
</div>
</div>
