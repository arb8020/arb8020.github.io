the best way to develop literacy in any domain is by doing comparisons of the same thing. coffee flights, cake tastings, side-by-side wine pours. its much easier to start to tease out whats different about them when you can compare directly.

i realized this when my girlfriend did a cake tasting event and i was attempting to teach food/art literacy through the form i made for it. the form prompted people to decompose their experience into components — texture, taste, aroma — evaluate each independently, then reason about how they interact. separate idea from execution. name the specific bite that stood out and why.

the same thing works for emotions. sharp/dull, heavy/light, warm/cold, discrete/diffuse. once you have words for the components, you can notice things you couldn't before.
