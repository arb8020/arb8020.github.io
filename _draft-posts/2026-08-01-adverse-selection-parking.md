in a competent market, you should expect that things that look too good to be true probably are. 

other cars parked in a busy street are counterparties with more information than you. an open spot in a packed street probably has a fire hydrant, a driveway, street cleaning hours, or some other reason it's open.

this actually ties nicely into why sometimes spots with signage are under-parked on average bc people read signs -> assume the spot is not legal. but frequently itll be for a different day or smth. now you are the shark.
