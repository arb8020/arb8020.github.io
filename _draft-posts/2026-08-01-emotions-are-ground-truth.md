its very easy to think and spiral. i eventually realized that i could justify anything with enough thought. and also that if i traced back my beliefs far enough it wasnt really clear which ones were original, adopted, or cargo culted. being able to 'resonate' with a belief by feeling it helped more.

thinking of emotions as:
1. somewhere in the body
2. describing them the way i might describe a physical object

sharp/dull, heavy/light, warm/cold, discrete/diffuse, etc.

the freedom to/from framing: you kind of have to feel the pain of freedom to, to willingly accept giving up freedom to in order to gain freedom from. i've been lifting more consistently and it makes me much more likely to give up freedom to sleep whenever i want in favor of getting enough sleep so my gym performance doesnt suffer.
