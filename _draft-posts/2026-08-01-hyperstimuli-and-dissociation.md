hyperstimuli require less being in the body to get the amount of dopamine/pleasure the user wants and are closer to numbing agents. they are so stimulating that you dont need to be there. your attention can be elsewhere and enjoy a fried chicken, not so for an almond. but with focus on the sensations of eating an almond it can be more pleasurable. in fact at its logical extreme one can find pleasure in simply being curious about one's own momentary experience. the breath is a common object here.

another good example is the progression from books -> movies/tv shows -> youtube -> short form. or even within youtube for example. some amount of video essays are literally this slop/dissociative content and netflix/other studios have literally admitted that people want shows that can be watched while scrolling on ones phone. on avg books require more focus/attention to imagine and understand and get the narrative out. older books even moreso. versus short form/etc.
