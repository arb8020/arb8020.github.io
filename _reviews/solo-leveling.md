overall: 3.5

not a full recommendation bc i feel like jinwoo's character is honestly pretty weakly written, but the fight scenes and the show overall are really well animated, and im honestly interested enough in the plot to see where things go and look forward to the next season. like the theme of being able to improve and adapt being super op but the character is 'special' in a way that still kinda bothers me - why is jinwoo chosen and not somebody else? also a lot of the plot feels a little 'things happen to jinwoo' than 'jinwoo makes things happen'

solid-good
