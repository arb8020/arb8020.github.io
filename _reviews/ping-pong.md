ep 1
the opening of the show seems to strongly hint at the themes we'll see around meaning and why people do what they do
it seems like the strength of this show is very much going to be its characters. already we see a ton of illustration of smile, peco, and kong. 
smile seems to simply not want to or not feel emotion, obviously in contrast to peco's much more playful and carefree nature. 
'hard work is for chumps with no talent' lol. really interesting idea here, very much reminds me of how myself and friends used to see school. like if you had to work hard that was almost undesirable bc it meant you weren't naturally talented enough. people would almost compete on how little they could get away with. 
i really liked kong's listening scene. i honestly could hear what they were describing after paying a bit more attention to it. the aggressive hit followed by the slower chop
i found it interesting that kong could immediately tell smile was holding back. i wonder why smile doesn't play as well as he could against peco. interestingly it seems the teachers couldn't tell, and just assumed peco was better. but kong seems to think smile is a level above, at least enough to challenge himself, even though he 11-0'd peco
kong's backstory of 'one slip up' causing him to end up in japan (??) is interesting. he seems to think peco just isn't talented? that one line about 'its a shame nobody instilled in him [smile] fighting spirit. but perhaps its more of a shame to believe you have talent when you dont (paraphrased)'
also the show is 11 episodes lol
i like the animation style. its very hand drawn and distinct
i wonder what the point of the repeated 'next stop? the one after' is. maybe its meant to be something about how peco and smile differ in temperament. peco is maybe a bit more impatient, focused on the next thing. but smile is more patient. 
the mean character who got demoted seems boring so far but maybe won't be as obviously flat later
not sure what to make of the teachers
insane shots all around though i can't really articulate any specific ones as much as i might like
